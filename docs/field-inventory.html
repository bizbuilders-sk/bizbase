Advanced source field map

Imported ClickUp schemas and read-only Pipedrive snapshots. The full map is an audit; column/filter/group flags describe explicit table registrations. Removed fields remain in read-only record history, never an Advanced menu. The audit below includes retired source descriptors for traceability; only fields marked Yes appear in table components; only explicitly registered business fields enter the table controls. Duplicates, task administration, unused source labels, raw financial metadata and detailed survey questions have no component registrations. The source archive remains available in Imported fields. Empty-only fields stay out of selectors. Company fields also follow the linked company on Contacts, Pipeline, Lead intake and Projects. Contact fields follow the linked contact on Pipeline and Lead intake. Numeric/date filters use the source values, never financial totals. With merged aliases, positive All conditions on the same source list must match one source record. IDs, BCC routing, access controls and photos are intentionally excluded. Unsupported or unpopulated formula values are not fabricated. Counts and activity dates are snapshots, not continuous synchronization.

Ownership rules

ConceptImplemented rule
Arrival datesFirst received is the earliest known original source or form receipt across aliases. It does not reset on migration, merge, qualification or later registrations. Added to BizBase uses local insertion time. Form received on is response-specific and follows the selected Event; inclusive date bounds support date ranges. Calendar dates use Europe/Bratislava. Imported records without historical evidence remain unknown rather than falling back to migration time.
Company factsOne Account master; linked context elsewhere. Submitted or reported values retain their own history.
Person factsOne Contact master; current email/phone are distinct from submitted values.
Company lifecycle and rolesLifecycle: Prospect / Customer / Former customer. Business roles: Client, Vendor, Partner, independently combinable.
Enquiry qualificationQualification belongs to the enquiry review. Retired company-fit fields are absent from Company and inherited table controls.
Marketing follow-up and sales stageMarketing follow-up belongs to a response and event; Stage belongs to an opportunity. They have explicit labels and do not silently synchronize.
SourcesSource system identifies provenance; Acquisition channel is a common business vocabulary; Form name / Form type / Discovery channel / Reported source have separate meanings.
OwnersIntake assignee, Sales owner and Project owner refer to one shared named directory, without creating login privileges. Legacy combined owner names are preserved as named assignments.
ServicesOpportunity and project scope reference one catalog. Only exact source name matches are reused automatically; other actual source products become explicit catalog records.
Financial amountsForecast, contract, monthly delivery revenue, vendor budget/actuals, invoices and cash are separate measures.
NotesSummary notes, original imported notes and dated activity notes have distinct labels. Original snapshots are retained.

One unambiguous reported company revenue range was promoted where available. Other source facts require explicit review. No ClickUp or Pipedrive record was modified. Existing financial values and source snapshots are retained. Gmail/Calendar integration and invoice/payment workflows remain separate work.
