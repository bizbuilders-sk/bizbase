BizBase fields and values

Current entity field dictionary and database population snapshot · 27 October 2026. Column, Filter and Group mean a field is eligible for that control; each person's saved layout and the data in the current view determine what is shown. Zero and No count as populated values. Dashes mean a field is linked, calculated, nested or imported, so this snapshot does not count it directly.

Companies own Lead status and Lifecycle. Contacts link to Companies; Deals and Projects have their own stages and values. Event responses stay attached to their source event and can be viewed from the related Company or Contact. Source-projected fields use the captured schema from 21 October; newly discovered source fields may differ. No individual CRM values are shown in this report.
